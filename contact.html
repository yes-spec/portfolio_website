FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you book for clients outside Africa?

Yes — the majority of our guests book from North America, Europe, Asia and the Middle East. We coordinate flights, visas guidance, and airport transfers regardless of where you're travelling from.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend 3–6 months ahead for popular parks and gorilla trekking permits, though we can often arrange trips on shorter notice — just ask.

What's included in the tour prices shown?

Listed "from" prices typically include accommodation, park fees, listed activities, and ground transport. International flights are usually excluded — your final quote will spell out every inclusion clearly.

Can trips be customised for honeymoons, families, or private groups?

Absolutely — every itinerary on our site is a starting template. Tell us your occasion, group size, and pace, and we'll tailor it accordingly at no extra planning fee.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept major international cards and bank transfers, with secure, staged payments (deposit plus balance before travel) so you're never paying the full amount upfront.
